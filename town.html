Your village

Grow from a keep and a dozen villagers into a town that can arm its own levies. Bandits will raid within a few minutes.

Build: pick a building at the bottom left, then click the ground. Woodcutters, quarries and mines must be near their resource; the ring shows how far they reach, and stone and ore run out.

People: newcomers settle while there is food to spare and the village is content; houses set the room, and burn firewood. Upgrades add work places; − / + sets how many work there. Paved roads speed walkers.

Chains: wood → planks → shields; flax → string; planks + string → bows; planks + feathers → arrows; ore + wood → iron → spears and swords.

Hauling: goods stay where they are made until someone carries them. Haulers from the keep and storehouses, and anyone without a job, carry crates to building sites, workshops and the barracks. Build storehouses near your workshops.

Levies: a barracks calls villagers up as soldiers when their kit has been carried there. Losses are villagers lost. Disband them to send them back to work.

Militia: while the village is attacked, Muster the militia (in the stock panel) calls every villager out with whatever is to hand. They are weak and break easily, but numbers can tip a close fight. Work stops until the attack ends; then they go back to their jobs. The dead are villagers lost.

Battle: click or drag to select, right-click to move or attack (twice to run there), right-drag to draw a line; Z runs everywhere. Ctrl+1–9 makes a group, 1–9 selects it; − / + sets the speed. Broken units run to the keep to regroup.

Camera: WASD, arrows or the screen edge to pan, Q / E to turn, scroll to zoom. Space pauses.

Sound: 🔊 in the top bar sets the volume or mutes. Credits for the music, sound and models.
