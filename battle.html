Controls

Left click / drag: select regiments (Shift or Ctrl/⌘ adds) · double-click: all of that kind

Right click: move there · on an enemy: attack

Right drag: draw the front line; length sets width. Drag left→right to face away from you

WASD / arrows or the screen edge: pan · Q / E or middle drag: turn

Scroll: zoom; zoom far out for the whole field

Space: pause · − / +: speed · Ctrl+1–9: make a group, 1–9: select it (twice: look) · H: halt · F: focus · Ctrl/⌘A: all

Formations: N line · T tight · L loose · V wedge · X schiltron · C charge · G hold · R fire at will (or the bar over the cards)

Before battle: place your regiments anywhere on the lit ground, then Begin battle (Enter).

Sieges: right-click your own wall or tower to man it. Right-click an enemy wall with a ram, ladder men or engines to work it; foot soldiers climb your ladders or hack at gates. Archers shoot further from walls and towers. Men on a gate or tower pour boiling oil on whoever is at its foot.

Mines: U (or Dig mine), then right-click an enemy wall: foot dig a mine from out in the field that brings the stretch down. When the enemy digs one under yours you'll hear it: select foot and press U to counter-mine. A garrison that can't hold asks for terms. ?weather=night: defenders sally in the dark.

Sound: 🔊 in the top bar sets the volume or mutes. Credits for the music, sound and models.

Spears stop cavalry charges from the front. Hit flanks and rear to break morale. Archers loose at will when idle. Broken units run to their keep and regroup. Men tire as they fight and run, so keep fresh regiments back; bowmen carry about two minutes of arrows and refill at camp.
