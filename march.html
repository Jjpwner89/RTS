Settings

How long a march takes

One place along the road is about 200 world units (the middle of the realm's roads: 174 to 314); two places about 400. Game minutes are the clock on the top bar; a season is five of them.

A raid, and the relief that answers it

A raid band sets out from its camp for the village. The village sees them leave and sends relief, after the reaction time below, from one place away and from two. The raiders have the village until relief is on them, then they break off and run for the camp; horse can run them down.
