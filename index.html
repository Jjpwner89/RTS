RTS prototypes

A medieval strategy game in progress: build and fight on one map, rising from a keep to a realm.

CampaignThe world map: four places, one rival house. Build up your villages, march armies and take Blackmoor. VillageBuild a village, arm levies from your own villagers, and hold off bandit raids. Battle testA field battle of about 1,200 soldiers: regiments, morale and routs. MarchfrontThe side project: armies, fronts and supply on one map.
