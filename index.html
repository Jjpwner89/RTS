Controls

Left click / drag: select regiments (Shift adds)

Right click: move there · on an enemy: attack

Right drag: draw the front line; length sets width. Drag left→right to face away from you

WASD / arrows: pan · Q / E or middle drag: turn

Scroll: zoom; zoom far out for the whole field

Space: pause · 1 2 3: speed · H: halt · F: focus · Ctrl/⌘A: all

Spears stop cavalry charges from the front. Hit flanks and rear to break morale. Archers loose at will when idle. Broken units run to their keep and regroup.
