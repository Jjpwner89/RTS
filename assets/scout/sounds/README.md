# Scouted sounds (2026-11-21)

Free sounds for gaps in `assets/audio`, found for the audio session to audition and wire. Nothing here is loaded by the game. **Everything is CC0**; no credit is needed. Each file's author, licence as shown on its page, and the page where it was read are in the table. Files are the originals (not trimmed, levelled or re-encoded); long ones want cutting into single hits or looping, as the existing sounds were.

Licences were read on each source page. **BigSoundBank**: every sound page says "License CC0 (public domain): Free and royalty-free", and the site's [licence page](https://bigsoundbank.com/droit.html) gives Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal, redistribution included; a credit ("Additional sounds: Joseph SARDIN - BigSoundBank.com") is optional. **OpenGameArt**: each page's License field shows CC0. HaelDB's pack there offers OGA-BY 3.0 or CC0 as alternatives; CC0 is taken.
