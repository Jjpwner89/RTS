EQUIPMENT
The exported packs contain original character/horse reference meshes and
animation, not replacement equipment. The review's simple tools, shield,
bow, sack, log and rock are contact guides only.

Grips are now PER CLIP. Each clip's animation.extras (and
wip/clip-manifest.json) carries weapon = {bone, grip, axis, ...}:
  bone  the wrist joint the prop rides on (Wrist.R, or Wrist.L for the bow).
  grip  grip centre in wrist-local coordinates, [0, 0.135, -0.035] for all.
  axis  wrist-local unit vector from the grip toward the weapon's head or
        point. It leans from the thumb side toward the fingers by a fixed
        angle per weapon: spear, hoe and lance 40 deg, axe and pick 25 deg,
        javelin and crossbow 30 deg, sword 20 deg, march spear 0 deg.
Do not use one global shaft axis for every clip; Codex's old rule
(right -X, left +X) only holds for diag 0.
Three.js sanitizes Wrist.R to WristR at load time; raw GLB names keep dots.

Other weapon fields:
  Spear_Thrust, Spear_Brace: lengthBehindGrip 0.91 (2.6 m reference spear).
    The left hand rides the shaft 0.42 m ahead of the right at guard; at
    full thrust the shaft slides through it to 0.22 m.
  Ride_Couch: lengthBehindGrip 0.9 (3.2 m reference lance).
  Chop, Mine: offhand -0.18 / -0.17 (left hand that far toward the butt).
  Hoe: offhand 0.22 (left hand toward the blade).
  Shield_Block, Shield_Wall: shield on Wrist.L, outward normal hand +Y.
  Bow_Loose: bow on Wrist.L; the right hand works the string.
  Javelin_Throw: hide or release the javelin at release-javelin (0.633 s).

Crossbow_Span: the crossbow is posed in MODEL space, never from hand roll.
  weapon.crossbow.held: rides at the right grip with the barrel toward +Z
    and the bow along +X, level from side to side (aimed at chin height).
  weapon.crossbow.grounded: nose-down for spanning, grip [0, 0.68, 0.36],
    barrel [0, -0.995, 0.0995], bow [1, 0, 0].
  Blend held -> grounded over blendToGround [0.18, 0.45] s and back over
    blendToHeld [2.10, 2.52] s. Events: nose-down 0.5, foot-in-stirrup
    0.57, string-spanned 1.35, bolt-laid 1.86, shoot 2.80. Animate the
    string and bolt on the equipment layer from those events.
wip/review.js shows exactly this blend and is the reference implementation.

RIDERS
Ride_Trot and Trot share a 1 s cycle; Ride_Gallop and Gallop share a 20/30 s
cycle. Start each rider/horse pair at phase zero and use identical time
scales. Riders are already posed at the normalized horse's seat height:
place their scene origins together, without adding a second saddle offset.
Ride_Idle, Ride_Couch and Ride_Slash are mounted actions based on the horse's
Idle pose. Ride_Couch ends held (LoopOnce + clampWhenFinished); for a moving
charge, blend its upper body over Ride_Gallop.

HOW THE ARMS ARE MADE
wip/make-clips.mjs regenerates all three packs deterministically.
Arms that hold something are keyed by upper-arm and forearm directions plus
the wanted weapon direction (foot-motions.mjs keyed(), rig-tools.mjs
Human.aimFk). aimFk picks forearm roll and wrist bend inside natural ranges
(roll +-80, flex +-50, deviation -30 toward the little finger to +15 toward
the thumb), so the wrist, forearm and elbow always agree; the weapon may
point a few degrees off rather than break a wrist. Large swings that
reverse the weapon (the chop, the pick, the sabre cut) pass through an
upright midpoint so the wrist never snaps.

VALIDATION (re-run 2026-11-20 on the reworked packs)
wip/validation-report.json: passes. Exact clip sets, 30 fps, finite
samples, unit quaternions, constant roots, closed loop poses, original
nodes, skins and bind data, no joint turning 75 degrees or more between
frames. Khronos glTF validator: zero errors (5 + 5 + 2 warnings, inherited
from the source skinned-mesh hierarchy).
The per-frame check caught forearm twists flipping 100-160 degrees in one
frame in Crossbow_Span, Javelin_Throw, Chop, Mine and Ride_Slash; those
are fixed. The fastest remaining joint changes (40-55 degrees in a frame)
are elbows and shoulders in the javelin throw, the chop and pick
downstrokes and the sabre cut, which are genuinely fast moves.
wip/joint-scan.mjs: every holding arm in range, except small leftovers
in left hands Codex placed by IK: Spear_Brace's left wrist reaches about
61 deg flex in one frame and Hoe's left about -50 deg deviation. Neither
looks wrong in the review.
wip/contact-checks.json: grip residuals of the arms still placed by IK.
Arms keyed through aimFk are not on it; they report aim error instead
(CLIP_DEBUG). Worst sustained aim error is about 18 degrees, with brief
peaks while a hand passes between holds (crossbow lowering, the cut).
Stale, from Codex's superseded clips, NOT re-run: wip/hand-validation.json
(its validate-hands.mjs encodes Codex's spear design, where both hands
move together) and wip/runtime-validation.json (the browser check on the
female rig). Re-check the female rig when the clips are wired in.
