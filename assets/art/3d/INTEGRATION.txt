EQUIPMENT
The exported packs contain original character/horse reference meshes and
animation, not replacement equipment. The review's simple tools, shield,
bow, sack, log and rock are contact guides only.
Default grip centre in wrist-local coordinates: [0, 0.135, -0.035].
Weapon/tool shaft runs along wrist-local -X on the RIGHT and +X on the
LEFT. This mirrors the source hands so the right thumb faces the weapon
head. For a review prop built along +X, rotate it pi radians about local Y
before attaching to Wrist.R. Spears, swords, javelins, axes, hoes, picks and
lances use Wrist.R. Bow and shield use Wrist.L.
For the reference shield, its outward normal is wrist-local +Y and its
face sits 0.08 m beyond the grip. The crossbow's vertical handle crosses
the palm on local X. Its stock orientation is authored in model space,
separate from wrist roll: in the ready/loaded pose the barrel faces +Z
and the bow spans +X, level from side to side. Follow those model-space
directions from the clip's weapon metadata while keeping its grip at
Wrist.R. Interpolate to the grounded nose-down orientation described below.
Do not inherit hand roll as crossbow roll.
Three.js sanitizes Wrist.R to WristR at load time; raw GLB names keep dots.

Spear_Thrust uses a second grip 0.29 m along the shaft. Reference spear:
2.6 m shaft with 0.91 m behind the right grip. At the end of Spear_Brace
that butt is grounded behind the back foot and the point faces upward
toward the horse's chest.
Chop and Mine use second grips 0.16 m / 0.15 m ahead of the right hand.
Hoe uses a second grip 0.18 m ahead, with the head approximately 1 m
ahead of the right grip. It follows ground level during the pull.
Bow_Loose's right hand controls the string; detach the arrow at its loose
event. Hide/release the javelin at its release event; Rout carries no gear.
Crossbow_Span needs a grounded equipment phase while BOTH hands span the
string. Blend into that phase from 0.30–0.45 s and out from 2.40–2.55 s.
Reference ground grip: [0, 0.68, 0.36], shaft direction [0, -1, 0.1]
normalized. Animate string/bolt separately using the supplied events.
This prevents the crossbow stock following the hands during the pull.
The bow, crossbow, arrow and projectile mechanics belong to the equipment
animation layer; no extra bones have been added to the character rig.

RIDERS
Ride_Trot and Trot share a 1 s cycle; Ride_Gallop and Gallop share a 20/30 s
cycle. Start each rider/horse pair at phase zero and use identical time
scales. Riders are already posed at the normalized horse's seat height:
place their scene origins together, without adding a second saddle offset.
The rider's pelvis, feet and rein hands follow a seat reference transformed
by the actual horse Body bone through the gait.
Ride_Idle, Ride_Couch and Ride_Slash are mounted actions based on the horse's
Idle pose. For a moving charge, blend the couched upper-body pose over
Ride_Gallop while leaving the mounted gait on the pelvis and legs.

Serve the RTS repository and open assets/art/3d/wip/review.html.
