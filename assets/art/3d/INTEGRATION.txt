QUATERNIUS MEDIEVAL ANIMATION CLIPS — BRIEFS 7.1 / 7.2
First delivery: Spear_Thrust, 2026-11-19.

clips-foot.glb retains the exact Male_Adventurer.source.glb node names,
hierarchy, rest transforms, skin joints, inverse bind matrices and source
geometry. Only the animation set and flat material colours are replaced.
No prototype body, replacement skeleton, or assets/models.glb edit.

Spear_Thrust: 24 intervals / 25 samples at 30 Hz, 0.8 seconds, one shot.
Start and end are the same guard. Root is constant. Body transfers weight
over a stepping left foot; right foot stays planted.

Equipment convention: parent spear to Wrist.R, position [0, .135, -.035]
in wrist coordinates. Shaft follows wrist-local +X. Preview shaft is 2.6 m,
with 0.9 m behind the grip. Wrist.L follows a grip 0.29 m farther along
the shaft. Bone names in GLB retain dots; Three.js GLTFLoader sanitizes
Wrist.R to WristR when it creates runtime objects and tracks.

Review: serve the RTS directory and open assets/art/3d/wip/review.html.
Includes original source body, a grip-reference spear, play/pause, timeline,
orbit controls. Backpack is hidden for the review, retained in the source
mesh for compatibility. Spear prop belongs to the review only.

Rebuild with Node.js and Three.js 0.170.0:
  THREE_MODULE=file:///path/to/three/build/three.module.js node wip/make-clips.mjs
Validate:
  THREE_MODULE=file:///path/to/three/build/three.module.js \
  GLTF_VALIDATOR=file:///path/to/gltf-validator/index.js node wip/validate-clips.mjs

Source character and horse: Quaternius CC0, as recorded in tools/models/README.md.
Animation authoring and review files live in assets/art/3d/wip/.
The Art workstream selects/bakes these into the game's models separately.
