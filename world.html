The campaign

You hold Ashford in the south. House Varn holds Blackmoor in the north. Between you lie two free villages, Hollin and Stowe, and Varn will not leave them free for long.

World map: click a place or army to select it, double-click a village of yours to go there. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, middle-drag or Q / E to turn.

Armies: in a village, select regiments and press March out, or muster the whole garrison from the map. Right-click a place to march there. Attacking a place or meeting an enemy army asks you to fight it out or auto-resolve.

Hauling: goods stay where they are made until haulers carry them. Arms must reach a barracks before a levy can be raised. Build storehouses near your workshops.

Villages keep working while you are away. Varn will come for yours after a few minutes; if you are elsewhere, the garrison fights on its own. Take a village and it becomes yours to build up.

Win by taking Blackmoor. M returns to the map from a village, and Space pauses anywhere.
