Paintings and interface

The portraits, house badges and banners, event pictures, map art and the parchment-and-ink interface were made for Hearth and Host with an AI image generator (OpenAI Codex), from the game's own written briefs. The soldiers' and horses' motions in assets/art/3d are reworked in this project from the animations of the packs above.

Ground textures

The photographed grass, earth, tracks, fields and rock in the ground are from Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain): leafy_grass, brown_mud_leaves_01, dirt_floor, farm_soil and aerial_rocks_02. See assets/textures/CREDITS.md.
