# Open questions for the creator

Tradeoffs where either answer plays well. The game carries on with the default named in each; answer here or in a questions thread and Realm will adjust.

## Rival tax rate (2026-11-19)

Rival houses tax their places at a third of your rate: 0.004 gold per person a second, against your villages' 0.012 at fair taxes (merchants get 1.5×; vassals already pay at your rate). It was set low on purpose after the playtests where rivals grew far too strong. Under "rivals follow the player's rules" they would tax as you do, so they would be richer, hire more sellswords and build their knights' halls sooner.

- **(a) Keep it** *(the default for now)*: rivals stay poorer than their land suggests, which keeps the early game gentle.
- **(b) The same rate as yours**: fairer and easier to read ("they have what their land gives"), but every rival roughly triples its tax income.
- **(c) Tie it to the Rivals option** on the title page: Gentle 0.004, Normal 0.008, Fierce 0.012.

Realm's lean is (c).
