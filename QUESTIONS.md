# Open questions for the creator

Tradeoffs where either answer plays well. The game carries on with the default named in each; answer here or in a questions thread and Realm will adjust.

## Militia muster (2026-11-20)

- **Should rival and free villages muster their people too?** Default: no. Their garrisons fight as they always have (free villages already turn out about 40 militia and bowmen). Yes would be fair and make every storm bloodier and harder, which slows conquest for you and for them.
- **Should calling the militia cost mood, or lift it?** Default: neither. The price is the lost work and the villagers who die. A mood cost would make it a last resort; a small lift ("they defended their homes") would reward a win.
- **A cooldown?** Default: none. It only works while the village is under attack, and stands down when the attack ends.
- **Should the governor call the militia out?** Default: only to save the keep, when an assault outnumbers the men standing; never for a raid. The alternative is never (you decide every time) or for any attack.
